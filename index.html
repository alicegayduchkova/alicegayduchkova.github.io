Featured Case StudyQuantitative Finance

Erie Indemnity (ERIE): Return & Risk Analysis

March–August 2026 return periods · Six monthly observations

Analyzed historical prices and dividend entries for one assumed Class A share, separating capital gains from current income to calculate total monthly returns.

Key finding: Returns varied substantially month to month despite an average close to zero; June was strongest (+17.80%) and April weakest (−12.38%).

Monthly total returns (%) · March to August 2026
Methods, interpretation & sources

Method: Total return = (ending price − beginning price + dividend income) / beginning price. Used the unadjusted Close prices and dividend entries documented in the course report.

Risk measures: Sample variance, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation (approximately 364.71). Because mean return was near zero, the coefficient of variation is unstable and should not be interpreted alone.

Source: Yahoo Finance historical data, as transcribed in the student's assignment. Source price data have not been independently reverified.

Franklin Templeton (BEN) vs. JPMorgan Chase (JPM)

2026 academic company research

Compared two financial services companies with different revenue drivers: investment management and a diversified banking, lending, and wealth management platform.

Research dimensions: Products, competitive landscape, ownership, reported revenue and profitability trends, growth opportunities, and market risks.

Key observations

Franklin Templeton: Considered the breadth of its investment products, reported assets under management, acquisition integration, and fee competition.

JPMorgan Chase: Examined diversification across consumer banking, lending, investment banking, and asset management, alongside exposure to interest rates and credit conditions.

Conclusion: Both were considered potential investments, subject to further valuation and risk analysis. This was qualitative research, not a completed valuation or buy recommendation.

Research sources: Company investor relations, SEC filings, and financial news sources listed in the original assignment.
