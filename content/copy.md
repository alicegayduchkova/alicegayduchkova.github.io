## Projects — "Projects I worked on"
Coursework where I researched companies, ran the numbers, and explained what they mean for an investor.

### Return and risk analysis of Erie Indemnity (ERIE) — Finance coursework, 2026
I calculated six months of total returns for one Class A share, splitting each month into price change and dividend income, then measured how much the returns varied.
- Average monthly return: 0.03%
- Standard deviation: 11.07%
- Monthly total return, % (Mar–Aug): −7.41, −12.38, −1.56, +17.80, −4.39, +8.12
- Source note: Prices and dividends from Yahoo Finance.
Takeaway: The average was close to zero, but single months swung from −12% to +18%. A flat average can hide a lot of risk, and a client needs that explained, not just reported.

### Franklin Templeton vs. JPMorgan Chase — Company research, 2026
I compared an asset manager that earns most of its revenue from fees on the assets it manages with a diversified bank that also lends and offers wealth management. I looked at products, competitors, revenue and profit trends, and risks.
Takeaway: Fee revenue moves with markets and fund flows, so keeping advisors and their clients invested matters a great deal.

### Matching investments to time horizon — Financial planning coursework, 2026
I sorted financial goals into short, medium, and long term, set savings targets, then revised them after looking at affordability, financing, and retirement needs.
Takeaway: Money you need soon calls for stability. Money you need in twenty years can take on more risk for growth.
